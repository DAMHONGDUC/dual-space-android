# Privacy policy

> **Owner input required before publication:** publisher legal name, support and deletion email, effective date, country-specific rights, and the public HTTPS URL of this policy. The app must not be submitted while this notice remains.

## Data stored on the device

Dual Space stores account names, colors, last-opened times, the package names of games the user adds, and app preferences (theme, language, launch confirmation, privacy lock, onboarding) in local app storage.

Each game copy runs inside Dual Space's own app storage. Game copies keep their app data, such as preferences, databases, files, and caches, in that storage. Dual Space does not read, modify, upload, or sell this data. Game copies share Dual Space's Android app sandbox; they are not a separate Android security boundary.

Local data stays on the device until the user removes an account or game in the app, uninstalls the app, or clears its data. Removing a game copy deletes that copy's app data from Dual Space's storage. The original game installed on the device is not affected.

## Services that process data

- **Google AdMob (free plan):** shows banner ads. Google may process device identifiers, diagnostics, approximate location derived from network information, and ad interaction data according to the user's consent choices. The app requests consent where required before loading ads and offers privacy options when applicable.
- **Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication (optional):** process the account identifier, display name, and email address when the user chooses to sign in.
- **RevenueCat and Google Play Billing:** process an app user identifier and purchase information to validate Premium access.
- **Google Play in-app updates:** process device metadata and the app version when the app checks for critical updates.

Dual Space does not use accessibility services, location spoofing, device-identity spoofing, macros, memory modification, or anti-cheat bypasses.

## Diagnostics

Release builds keep only event names and error types in the device log, not game names, account names, or identifiers. The optional diagnostic report is shared only when the user chooses to share it. It contains the app version, Android version, device model, and readiness state, and it contains no credentials or game data.

## Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their Dual Space sign-in account in **Settings → Delete account**. Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and signs out of RevenueCat. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription, which users manage in Google Play. It does not remove local game copies, which users remove in the app.

Users who no longer have the app installed can request deletion at the deletion page and support email listed at the top of this policy. Purchase records that must be kept for tax, fraud, or legal reasons are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own retention policies.
